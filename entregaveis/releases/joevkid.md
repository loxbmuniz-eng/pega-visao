Joevkid is a Brazilian DJ and music producer based in London. Since 2014, he has
been exploring the boundaries of electronic music with a unique fusion of
Progressive House, Techno, and bold touches of Psytrance. His productions carry a
captivating, progressive, and hypnotic sonic identity, a true musical journey.

Founder of Atipic Records, Joevkid is also a talent curator and a visionary of the
underground scene, creating space for authentic artists and sounds that break away
from the ordinary. His label is a reflection of his artistic vision: breaking
boundaries and delivering music with purpose and personality.

On stage, Joevkid brings energy and connection. He has performed at legendary
venues like Ministry of Sound and is a resident of Fox In The Box at Union Club in
London. His sets are intense, sensorial, and precisely crafted, experiences that
turn dance floors into immersive atmospheres.
